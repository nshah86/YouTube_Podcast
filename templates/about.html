About

VideoTranscript Pro turns YouTube videos into transcripts, AI summaries, and podcast MP3s. Built for researchers, students, podcasters, and teams who learn from video but prefer reading or listening on the go.

How it works

  • Extract — captions pulled straight from YouTube (free).
  • Summarize — GPT reads the transcript and writes a structured summary (paid).
  • Podcast — the summary becomes a two-host conversation, rendered to MP3 (paid).
